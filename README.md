# Research on Small Business Sustainability in Kazakhstan

A mixed-methods research study analyzing the core internal and external factors contributing to the early closure of small businesses in Kazakhstan.

##  Overview
The project investigates the primary reasons behind small business failure during their initial years of operation, combining statistical/quantitative analysis with qualitative case studies and interviews.

##  Key Topics Covered
- **Financial Dynamics:** Cash flow management and access to capital.
- **Operational & Regulatory Environment:** Administrative barriers and supply chain stability.
- **Market Strategy:** Niche saturation and preliminary market analysis.
